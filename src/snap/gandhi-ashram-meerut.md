Its an Ashram in Meerut, India, which was built in 1920 to support *Swadesh* and *Non-Violence* moment.

It also acted as a small Khadi factory to hand build India’s own national flag.

Now after 90+ years its just a ruin but still standing here and imagining it full of Indian freedom fighters including **Ghandhiji** and **Nehru**—first prime minister—make me feel as if it were yesterday.
