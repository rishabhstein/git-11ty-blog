This book is a collection of nine short stories written by Indian American author *Jhumpa Lahiri*, exploring the life of migrated Indian families in USA. These stories give you a window to see in the life of Indian families living in America, especially the cultural shock, loneliness, and their struggle to adopt the American life. 

A daily life of immigrant might be boring for most people but when you move thousands of kilometers far from home country (unwillingly, just to survive) and whole world see your culture as an alien one then it matters. Thats what I liked about the book as it tries to capture this alien-ness Indian people feel in different countries, especially during 60-70s time. Now situation is bit better as we have advance communication technology.

The author won Pulitzer prize for the book. However, out of nine stories at least two tested my patience. Those were felt prolonged without any major conclusion. I wont reveal their name to not to spoil other's experience.

I recommend it reading at least once. So **3 stars**.
