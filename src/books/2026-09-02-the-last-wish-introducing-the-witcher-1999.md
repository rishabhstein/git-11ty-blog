If you have read my [review (comments) of the TV series *The Witcher*](/movies/1767217422915/), you know my disappointment for *Henry Cavil* leaving after third season. Following that I decided to read the original source (this book) so that I can see the real Witcher from the book. And may be it will help me feel a bit better.

The series follow the story of half human half magical being *Geralt of Rivia* who is also known as *The Witcher*.  He used to be a human as a little child but he was abducted and converted to a Witcher. So it is a type of being in this magical universe, along with Sorcerers, Demons, Djinns, Elfs and so on. This book is the first in the series where the author introduce *The Witcher* universe. 

I am not a fan of this genre (due to my short attention span, ADHD) but I was overall happy with the book. It took me some time (may be 3-4 months) to finish but whenever I was reading it I was enjoying it. Author kept it nicely balanced between scenery details and pace of the story. By not giving superfluous amount of scenery details, he made it ADHD friendly :)

I am unsure whether I will be reading next book in the series any time soon but I look forward to it. 

I recommend it with **4 stars**.
