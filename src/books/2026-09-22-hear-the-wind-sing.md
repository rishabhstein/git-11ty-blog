This is the second book of *Murakami* I am reading, which I finished before finishing the first one. I was impressed by his writing style while reading his memoir where he mentioned about this book. So I decided to check his first creation as a writer.

It's a short book without any concrete story. The protagonist discusses mundane events of his daily life, such as meeting with friends and dating girls. So overall it is a bit boring. However, whats amazing is that it showed how *Murakami* build himself from this writing style to what he is known for. That's why I have read it.

So you can read it if you are free and looking for passing time. I recommend it with **3 stars**.
