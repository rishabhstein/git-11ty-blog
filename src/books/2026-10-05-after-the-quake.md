A short collection of stories by [Haruki Murakami](https://notes.rishabhps.com/posts/haruki-murakami/)  written around a big earthquake that destroys Tokyo. In all the stories characters struggle through their regular life problems and emotional issues, where the tides turn when the earthquake hit the city. Despite the tragic situation, Murakami managed to keep characters positive with thoughtful endings. 

There is also a story, the best one in my view, where a frog tries to convince a lonely hard-working man that his morals are important to save the world from earthquake, but ending is surprising. So to know the ending, I encourage you to read the book. It's a short one (less than 200 pages), and I finished it in around two hours by listening it on self-hosted [Kokoro](https://kokoroai.org/) text to voice agent.

Despite the positives, the break in story made the book a bit boring, so I wont pick up again second time. I can only give it **3 stars**.
