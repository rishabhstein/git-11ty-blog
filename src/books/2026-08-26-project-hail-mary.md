This book is, as my friend told me, indeed a page turner. Its almost 500 pages long but I finished it in a week—one of my fastest read ever. The story is similar to the movie of the same name ([yeah, I saw the movie first](/movies/1776287786641/)). But I enjoyed the book more than the movie. This book is amazing. The friendly alien *Rocky* is cuter and smarter in the book. As a reader I got attached to him as the main character of the book, Dr. Grace did.

I highly recommend it with *5 stars*.
