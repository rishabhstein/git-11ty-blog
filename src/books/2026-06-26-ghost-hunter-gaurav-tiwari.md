I remember during my college years (10 years ago) hearing about so called ghost hunter, Gaurav Tiwari. He died in his bathroom after some year in unclear circumstances. This book tells us his story, and describes many cases he and his team solved. Reading them gave me chills.

What I liked about it is a lack of bullshit, despite of it being from a genre in which stories are usually fictional. This is not fiction and yet writer managed to keep the story telling balanced with occasional exaggerations.

Its cool to read. I recommend it with **3 stars**
