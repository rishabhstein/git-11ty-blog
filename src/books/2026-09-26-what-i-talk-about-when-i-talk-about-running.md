This is a memoir of *Haruki Murakami*, I mentioned about in the previous [book post](/books/2026-09-22-hear-the-wind-sing/). Here the author describes his journey of becoming a novelist from a bar owner and how sport activities, especially running, helped him in doing so. He became a serious long distance runner in his early thirties while prior to that time he was rarely active. So reading his journey was inspiring. The author revealed many nuances for amateur runners that can be helpful in developing long-distance running habit—at least it was helpful for me. For instance:

> The main thing was not the speed or distance so much as running every day, without taking a break.

It is a short book so one can finish it in a single read. However, I took it slow because I did not want this book to end. Reading it inspired me and gave me a new understanding of long-distance running. I highly recommend it with **4 stars**.
