It's a remake of the legendary animated [TV series](https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/246-avatar-the-last-airbender?language=en-US) of the same name. Netflix is known for ruining TV series so there are no surprises. I was ok with first season but S02 is even worse. All the actors did a fine job but the choice of selecting scenes is weird. In the original series, there are so many inspiring scenes but in Netflix series director chose mostly the odd ones. The result is a story with fast pace and loosely connected incidents.

I am a fan of animated version so its worth watching at least once. However it only deserves **3 stars**.
