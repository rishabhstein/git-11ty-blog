Its three decades old movie with a non linear storyline made of a few seemingly unrelated stories of a hitman, a boxer, a lunatic couple and two gangsters.

It felt unique, funny but weird. Later I found out this was first of its kind in Hollywood, which made it so popular. First time watching was ok but i need to watch it multiple times to see its strength. So I will give **4** stars.
