It's a *Netflix* series based on the books of the same name by *Andrzej Sapkowski*, which was going very well till season three, when the lead actor was *Henry Cavill*. However, he quit after season three without making any fuss, and sadly, the following season came out crappy. I am complaining because I watched all four seasons in a row, and from the beginning of the fourth season, the series is completely off. The quality is so bad.

No one knows the reason why Cavill quit the show in the middle, as he was excited in the beginning and was ready to commit to all seasons. He was a huge fan of the game series *The Witcher* and the book, and he played the role of a Witcher so well that *Andrzej Sapkowski* said that he was imagining the Witcher exactly the way Cavill was playing. There were rumours about producers being unhappy with Cavill's seriousness for the role as she could not adapt the show for the American audience (WTF). The producer literally blamed the American audience [here](https://geektyrant.com/news/the-witcher-showrunner-responds-to-fan-backlash-over-straying-from-the-books?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

Imagine, same talented cast, a well-developed and successful story, but the producers are less talented than the lead actor.  The result is a disaster. Now that Cavill gone, the producer did what she wanted, and she fucking ruined it. Thank you, *Netflix*.

To honor Cavill's performance, I will give *5⭐* for seasons 1-3, and for crappy producers *2⭐* for season four.
