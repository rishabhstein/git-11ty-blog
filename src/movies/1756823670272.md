A few days ago I was reading about sharks from Jurassic era. So I decided to watch this movie and the following part. But first one is already so disappointing that I am unsure to watch second one.

There were many flawed bullshit in this movie such as using a aeroplane like submarines to go to 6000m deep. What a joke!! The pressure would be so high at this depth that the glass needed for that machine will be too thick and too heavy, making this design worse than the design of **Oceangate** subs.

In addition to scientific mistakes, there were lots of boring and predictable moments. So I can only give this movie **2/5 stars**.
