I rarely watch Bollywood movies now a days as they show typical stories without any original ideas. But as I am in India now so I decided to go whatever was in cinema.

Today's movie is **War-2** which is a sequel of [War](https://www.themoviedb.org/movie/585268-war) movie.  I was unaware of it and I came to know when my wife told me.

The lead actor, **Hritik Roshan's** acting was a big joke. The opening scene, a fight with Japanese mercenaries, made me laugh as he seemed to have know real experience with Japanese swords. In most of the action scenes, the background music--death metal or rock--was too loud, almost deafening.

Overall, the movie was a disaster with bad acting, overly loud music, ok-ish story. I will only give **2/5 stars**.
