After finishing two seasons of *The Witcher*, I was so excited about the show that all I wanted to do was hear and think about it all the time. Tempted, I decided to watch Blood Origin.

It's a prequel with only four episodes. The timeline of this show is way back in time, before Geralt’s existence. This world belongs to *dwarves* and *elves*, where elves are ruling and fighting each other. The magic is limited to a few elven mages—there are no humans in this world—among whom most prefer not to work with the rulers, except one mage. This odd mage is lowborn, so he tries hard to earn status and power, resulting in him exploring the dark side of magic and leading to the events mentioned in *The Witcher*.  This series tries to explain mainly the elven history and how *dwarves, humans, monsters, witchers, and elves* come to live in this world.

I am glad that despite the bad reviews, I gave it a try. It’s not as good as the original show, but I am happy with it, and I recommend it with **3/5 stars**.
