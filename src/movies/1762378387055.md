This movie I watched almost a month ago, and the emotions it stirred were deep. Every time I was thinking to write about it, the words were not justifying my emotions (or my English isnt good enough). Thats why I am a month late. Oopsy.

> **Summary:** A young boy in 13th century Scottish village grows up to be a strong man, among turmoil and tragedies of Scottish freedom movement. Despite all the chaos, he travels and explores from England to Rome, and finally decides to go back to Scotland to live a peaceful life with his beloved girlfriend. But destiny takes its turn and throws him in the middle of Scottish English war, where he becomes a legend known as **Willim Wallace**.

This is a story of bravery, courage and back stabbing. Wallace has no interest in war or politics, and neither in ruling. He does what time demands—to help people. But people being people; the cunning rats betray him at the very moment when they can get independent Scotland. A shame!!

A must watch. It deserves **5/5 stars**.
