You might know about my fascination with ancient Rome. I mentioned it in a few posts ([here](https://sigmarootpi.com/posts/2025-06-30-capital-museum-rome/) and [here](https://sigmarootpi.com/posts/2025-06-23-wisdom-from-roman-empire/)) about my visit to Rome in May last year. I love to read about it in books or to see it materialized in movies or in TV series. This TV series, about which I came to know in a Reddit post, is a gem. I loved it. The makers did a remarkable job by developing a storyline that revolves around real historical events. From the streets of ancient Rome to the dresses of Roman soldiers and the senates, everything is carefully designed to remain close to historical knowledge of ancient Rome. One particular example is the dress of *Cato the Younger*. In history, Cato was known to live aescetic life as a **Stoic**. So when he became senator of the Roman Republic, he refused to wear fancy *Togas*—a dress Roman senators used to wear—and chose a black dress made of everyday material. In the series, he is alone, wearing that black dress, indicating the effort makers put in making it. I was so amazed. Alas, they (makers) only made two seasons out of the planned five.

I highly recommend it. It deserved **5**⭐.
