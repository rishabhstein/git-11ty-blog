I am always down for a good sport movie. This one is unique as it breaks a stereotype in India that wrestling is only for men.

Its about a former wrestler **Mahavir Singh Phogat** who trains his daughters for Olympics. Both daughters in the beginning resist but later understand the struggle of their father and accept the wrestling career.

Why it is important? The reason is *child marriage*. Yes in few states of India this problem still exist. So by breaking the stereotype *Mahavir* showed that girls are no less than boys, and girls are not only to be get rid of.

It deserves **4/5 stars**.
