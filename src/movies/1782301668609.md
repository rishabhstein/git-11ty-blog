A follow-up movie and finale of the series [The Last Kingdom](https://blog.rishabhps.com/movies/1782301211644/), which I finished a day after watching the series. The story follows the story of the grandsons of *Alfred the great*, and their rivalry to become the king of united England. Uthred the warrior, now an old man, is again dragged in the war.

Its one time watch, especially for those who loved the series. So I recommend it with **3stars**.
