More I watch [Shahrukh Khan’s](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah_Rukh_Khan) movies more I admire him. After all he is known as *King of Romance*. This movie is about an Indian tour guide, Harry, living in Europe after running from his home many years ago. In one of his tour he is forced to help her client *Sejal* after finishing the tour. In the beginning he does it unwillingly but later as they spend more time together, they start developing feelings for each other.

Khan’s ability to deliver deep romantic scenes without even being physical is truly amazing. Ofcourse the musical score helps a lot here. The main [score](https://youtu.be/cYOB941gyXI) is so beautiful that I was humming it even after many hours.

It deserves **4**⭐️.
