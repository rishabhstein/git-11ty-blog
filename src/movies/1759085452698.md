This TV series was a delightful treat for my mind—Slow and beautiful. You can imagine it from my [comment](/microfeed/2025-09-25-pride-and-prejudice-s01e05/) on 4th episode:

> Oh dear. I felt so beautiful. My heart melted when Lizzy’s mind changed completely for Mr. Darcy. Ofcourse, I felt that money is a key factor but neither Mr. Darcy nor Lizzie give priority to money. The peak of beauty was the ending scene when Miss Elizabeth Bennet looked back at Mr. Darcy from the   carriage… oooo😍😍

Yesterday after finishing the series I could not stop smiling. I wanted to feel those moment with Lizzie and Mr. Darcy a bit longer. So I downloaded an *Ebook* as well as an *Audiobook*. I plan to read/hear it now.

I highly recommend it if you want to watch something nice, cute, and sincere family drama. I think it deserves **4/5 stars**
