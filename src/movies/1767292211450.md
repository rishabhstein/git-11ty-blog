First movie of the year, and I chose to watch this dystopian movie. This one is a rewatch, and I wanted to watch it from the past few weeks. I have no idea why, as this one is not my favorite movie. So, the only reasons I can think of are that I saw it a long time ago, and I wanted to watch something that could shake me and help me see deeper into life, especially around the new year.

Our world is always chaotic, but humans have developed technology to exploit nature further and isolate themselves (psychologically) from the consequences. However, in reality, the danger is always there, waiting for the right time, no matter whether we are doomscrolling or binge-watching Netflix. So the climate crisis finally hits in the movie, and the world needs to face the wrath of nature. Politicians need to listen to scientists before it's too late—a bitter truth.

It's a survival movie, so not much to say except some world of appreciation. The movie managed to ignore the blood involved moments by hiding them, making it less disgusting. It also tried to give some moral lessons, such as *keeping promises* and *doing your duty, honestly*.

Overall, it deserves **3 ⭐ **.
