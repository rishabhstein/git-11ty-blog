I wanted to watch this movie for a long time, but due to overwhelming mental work, I was postponing anything that was intellectually demanding. But finally I had enough rest (2+ months) and I felt better, so I thought to watch it today.

**The Man from Earth: Holocene** is a sequel to the movie **The Man from Earth** which was intellectually stimulating.  So obviously, there was hype for the second part. This movie managed to give the same awe feeling as its previous part. However, the storyline was weak. *David Lee Smith* played *John Young* as charismatic as in the previous part, but other characters were a bit silly, especially college students.

Anyway, I dont want to criticise it much as I have really enjoyed it. Of course, I was missing the college professors' discussions as in the first part, but still, it was worth watching at least once. It deserves at least **3/5 stars**.
