I watched this movie yesterday. The storyline is very simple:

> An accountant goes to Oakland to join a new job. But soon he finds himself involved in a double murder incident. Running for his life, chased by assassins and police, he meets a *native Indian* who guides him till the end of his life.

It's an artistic movie, shot in artistic black and white. The story moves slowly, and I got bored a few times, but I pushed myself to finish it. In the end, it was rewarding as the movie left me ruminating about many philosophical questions.

If you expect a highly stimulating movie, unfortunately its not for you. However, if you like philosophy and art, you are going to like it. I recommend it with **4** stars.
