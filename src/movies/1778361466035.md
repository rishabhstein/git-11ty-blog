I have seen so many documentaries on Einstein‘s genius, his amazing research work, and his Eureka moments, but this one was unique because it covers the emotional side of Einstein, specially during the war.

It is hard to imagine Einstein was kicked out of his  Home, and how his own people were against him. They  rejected his scientific work and called it a Jewish scam. Woww. 

Later when he sailed to US, he never came back. How sad. Its worth watching once. **3stars**
