Regardless of its religious importance, this movie was overhyped. Most of Indians know the storyline from childhood so that wasnt a surprise. However, from makers side CGI was bad, almost like a videogame.

Intensive and aggressive music gave this movie a *Tollywood* touch, as a result it became a superhero like movie where Demons and Gods fight each other for dominance.

I left this movie after being bored halfway. So I will give it only 2/5 stars.
