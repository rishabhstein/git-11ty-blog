A science teacher goes to space to save our sun. In this journey he meets a friendly alien who also came for the similar reasons. Ofcourse, the alien speaks a different language so they need to figure out a way to communicate and to face awkward problems.

The movie is full of nice and creative humor. I like that the alien is not portrayed as scary similar to other sci-fi movies. Alien is just another living being from a different planet.

I highly recommend it. **5 stars**
