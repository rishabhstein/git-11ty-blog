A weird crime cinema, at least for modern times, amalgamating criminal mind with Samurai code. That's exactly the theme of the movie.

A professional killer, alias Ghost Dog, strictly follows Samurai code. He is a big fan of Japanese culture, and an avid reader who repeatedly quote many times from a book *Hagakure*—a practical and spiritual guide for a warrior written in nineteenth century—throughout the movie. 

The story line is simple, a killer eliminating people who want to kill him, and yet it leaves a deep impact. I think one need to watch it again to understand it properly. I recommend it with **4 stars**.`
