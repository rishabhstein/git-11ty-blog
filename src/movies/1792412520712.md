A young man, *Joe*, whose fiance died in a homicide just few days before the wedding tries to cope up with grief and emotionally supports his parents in-law. The whole incident unfolds slowly through conversations where the trio (Joe and in-laws) go on with their lives. However, there is a secret between *Joe* and his dead fiance which he is afraid to tell in order to avoid hurting the in-laws.

The whole journey of dealing with grief, being empathetic, staying strong and showing courage in the tough times makes this movie therapeutic  for the viewers. The pace of the story is slow, making it a bit boring in the beginning. However, the patience bore fruits, and I felt more connected to the emotions of *Joe* and grieving in-laws as the story developed. Despite the heaviness of the incident, the ending leaves the viewer with sweetness of love and life

This movie should be watched many times. I recommend it with **5 stars**.
