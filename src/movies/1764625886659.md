The story line of this movie is set in 60-70s on some other Earth. Fantastic four are celebrated superheroes and people love them. Then one day, on this happy planet, *Silver-Surfer* arrives, which means *Galactus* is coming. The rest of the movie follows *Fantastic Fours* planning to defeat the Galactus.

It was a nice movie but the biggest disappointment for me was female Silver-Surfer. Dont get me wrong. I love Silver surfer from childhood. I used to watch it on Cartoon Network. So I was expecting a male one.

I think the focus of this movie was more on setting up the stage for **Dr. Doom** and **MCU phase 6** than having a solid story line of its own. So I will only give it **3**⭐️
