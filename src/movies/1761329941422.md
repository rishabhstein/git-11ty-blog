I watched this movie last week (sorry for always posting it late) and I was very impressed by it especially when I have low expectation from American directors about the portrayal of Paris. Surprisingly, Woody Allen did a remarkable job here. The movie has very light and relaxing vibes. I was hooked from beginning to the end.

It is about an American newbie writer who comes to **Paris** with his fiancee. The writer is fascinated by historical figures and legendary artists of pasts. He is a thinker who ruminates and finds it hard to express his thoughts, making it hard for his fiancee to understand him. This might be the reason why they both look incompatible from the beginning, completely different personalities.

One day he goes for a walk in late evening, and while wondering in Paris around midnight meets a group dressed elegantly in 80s style. This meeting gives him a chance to meet many historic figures such as **Pablo Picasso** and **Salvador Dali**. I should stop spoiling further but this meeting changes his life and gives him a chance to reconsider his life choices.

I do not think that the writer can be played by someone else but *Owen Wilsons*. A big shout out to **Rachel McAdams** for doing one more time-travelling movie. She is a face of time-travel movies, and I love that.

Finally my ratings, I think it deserves **4 stars**.
