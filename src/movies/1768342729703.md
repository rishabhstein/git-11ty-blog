> A seemingly psychologically ill man with his friend try to save humans from an alien species from Andromeda galaxy.

I went to watch this movie on Sunday without having any prior knowledge about the storyline. I did not even check the trailer, so I had not much expectations. From the beginning, movie was full of suspense. It took me more than half time to understand whats going on. And even then it was unclear whether the protagonist was psychologically ill or not? He was shown to believe in conspiracy theories and alien invasion—ofcourse this indicates that he was indeed  psychological ill. But was he??

For the uniqueness perspective alone, I liked the movie very much. It kept me on my toes till the end. But I dont like bloody scenes. So I will give this movie **4**⭐️
