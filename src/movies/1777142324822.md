It was a rewatch after I saw [Einzelganger's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ER69AuimtOQ) from last year about the philosophy of **Tyler Durden**, one of the main character, and my favorite one.

This movie is a classic cult, and has a big fan following. I expect that you must have already watched it at least once. So I wont go in the details of the plot.

However, what is amazing here is that thirty years later one can still connect to it. The movie was made in 90s when capitalism was not at its peak—people might had thought otherwise—compared to today, but the whole storyline can be imagined even in todays scenario. Gen-Z can connect to it even more.

I would love to see a follow up of the movie where Tyler will blow off all the new data centres and AI companies. 

This movie deserves **5 stars**.
