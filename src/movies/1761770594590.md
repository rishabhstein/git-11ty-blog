In my previous movie posts, I think I gave many spoilers. So I have decided that from now on the storyline will be a bit more brief.

> **Summary**: It's a story of a spoiled British boy, Jamie, born to rich parents, living in China before Japan invaded during WWII. After the invasion, life becomes difficult for him because during the chaos, he gets separated from his parents. To survive, he needs to learn everything from scratch. The whole movie is about his journey, which is inspiring and sometimes deeply emotional.

It's one of the popular **Steven Spielberg** movies where the highlight of the movie is young **Christian Bale** who played Jamie, and proved his acting talent even as a kid. He single-handedly carried the movie on his shoulder. I felt hooked from the beginning, even though there is not much story—it's just a survival movie. The chemistry of the duo **Bale** and **John Malkovich**, who played Basie, an American expatriate scammer in China, is so natural that whenever they come on screen, I feel deep emotions.

On the downside, the movie is long and sometimes boring. It could be my ADHD mind, but I was losing focus in some scenes.

Overall, it's an amazing movie. I recommend it with **4 stars**.
