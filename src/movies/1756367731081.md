This movie is a remake of the classic **War of the worlds** by *H. G. Wells* but the storyline adapted the idea of dystopian modern survilance technology where an american secret agent sitting behind computer can access everything--including cams, microphones, and social media--around us.

He is also a typical father who stalks his son, his daughter and her boyfriend. All his kids are irritated due to his behavior. Later some top-secret plan leads to alien attacks and agent's family helped him save the world.

This movie can also be considered a family drama, a modern Bollywood, which makes it worth watching at least once. I will give **3.5/5 stars**.
