This is a fascinating documentary which adds an emotional touch to the life of our ancestors, *Homo Neanderthals*, who were previously assumed to be dumb. *Ralph Solecki's* while studying *Shanidar caves* proposed that Neanderthals also had a complex social behavior. 

His interpretation of flower burial seemed to me a bit extreme. Nevertheless the direction he took was right.

 I highly recommend this documentary. **Rating 4.5/5**
