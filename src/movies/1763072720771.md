I am completely new to **Predator** series so please ignore my ignorance.

> **Summary:** An alien hero on an alien planet gets inspired by the story of alpha wolf and decides to save an intelligent humanoid and an alien monkey. The villains are other humanoids.

This movie is *John Wick’s alien version*. The storyline is inspiring as the protagonist is on his own journey where he needs to learn cooperation and friendship. He fights and fights, and fights, finally saving his pack and taking his revenge. The alien monkey likes him and do some weird alien stuff.

What pissed me off is that alien John Wick’s face is badly designed according to Physics. He has no ear lobes so hearing is bad, his eyes are bit deeper which makes his viewing angle small. I am not sure he can be a predator as its easy to hunt him compared to the alien monkey.

Its one time watch, ans deserves **3/5 stars**
