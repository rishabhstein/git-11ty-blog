Its a biographic movie of **Michael Jackson** which takes you behind the scene in his real life. He was talented from his early childhood, and so does his four brothers—basically whole Jackson family. His father trained them all, but he was strict and controlling. Later he broke free from his controlling father, and became the legend we know him today for.

Tbh, its a family drama and nothing more. I only liked those parts where the inspiration for his popular songs were shown.

A one time watch with **3 stars**
