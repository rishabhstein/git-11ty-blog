I feel bad that I have not heard about *The Odyssey* earlier but thanks to *Nolan* as he made a movie about it.

The storyline is taken from a famous ancient Greek epic composed by **Homer** about a man *Odysseus* who was forced to go on a war, leaving his wife and new born son. The journey costs him twenty years to come back. In this journey he meet many mythological creatures such as one eyed giant, and face wrath of Poseidon, the sea God.

What I liked about the movie is that Nolan managed to keep the lessons of moral and ethics, along with human struggles of Odysseus in a story made up with gods and witches. I mean he could have make it as a plain fictional movie focused on magic and myths, but he did not. Its a welcome approach.

I highly recommend it. It deserves **5 stars**
