I read the book with the same title last month but since then, almost everyday, I was missing its characters. Yesterday I decided to watch this movie to feel a little nostalgia.

The storyline was almost similar to the book but many deeper moments from the book were missing. May be its impossible to give similar depth to the movie characters as in books.

Portrayal of *Amir*, the protagonist, was most accurate while other characters loosely resembled the book characters. For instance, *Hasan* seemed in the movie a side character while that wasn't the case in books. I also liked the father of *Amir* who was an honest and ethical man, and the actor played it nicely. 

Overall, the movie does stirs emotions at important occasions, similar to the book, but with slightly less intensity. So I liked watching it once. It deserves **3.5/5 stars**
