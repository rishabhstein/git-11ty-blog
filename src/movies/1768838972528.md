I watched this movie on a flight. The title of the film itself was intriguing, but I was not expecting much from it. In the past few years, I have developed an interest in lighter movies—thanks to Netflix—where the stimulation strings are not too much pulled.

> Its about an English teacher who moves to Argentina during chaotic times. He saves a penguin from dying and unwillingly keeps it with him. With penguin, he and the whole school change similarly to when, after seeing an emotional mirror, people change.

I love this movie for its genuine (event-based on real life) story. Also, it was inspiring how another species—which is not a cat or a dog—so easily adapts to humans, especially when humans are kind.

The ending is sad and beautiful. I recommend it with **5**⭐.
