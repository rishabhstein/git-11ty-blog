This was my first time when I saw so many details of the life of *Jesus of Nazareth*. I knew about him as a religious figure but not as a real historical character. This series brings him alive. Everything about this series is amazing. The actor who played Jesus did it so well that the whole time it really feels like you are with Jesus.

This historical drama covers many stories of his followers, his encounter with the Roman Empire, and much more. I am not a Christian but I do see the beauty in his deeds--how much he has done for his followers and the world. He reminds me of many spiritual gurus who existed in most cultures and dedicated their lives to humanity.
**5 stars**.
