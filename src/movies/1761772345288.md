It's a French movie which I watched in English, but part of the English version was still partly in French ;).

> **Summary:** A young woman, Agathe, in modern-day France, is struggling to bring out her voice as a female writer. She is a big fan of the legendary **Jane Austen**. One day, her friend sent the first chapter of her novel as an entry to a competition which was organized by the great-great-grand nephew of *Jane Austen*. The Austen family loves the first chapter and invites her to spend two weeks with the family to further write her novel. Here she discovers her true love and writing spirit.

It is supposed to be a comedy movie, but I could not understand French humor very well. That doesn't mean it's bad. It is a slow and relaxing movie, which is good for a one-time watch. The protagonist, *Agathe*, is very emotional but also very good at hiding it. This might be why she is good at understanding the importance of human emotions in literature. The glimpse of it can be seen in discussions among writers at the *Jane Austen residency*, where *Agathe* seems to be the only one focused on literature over scientific-political writings.

I partly disliked the movie, but I also understood the depth of it. So I will give it a balanced **3 stars**.
