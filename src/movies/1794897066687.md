Dog movies are often cute, emotional, and beautiful but their numbers are limited. This  movie is another asset in that collection. However, this outperforms most of the previous movies and goes in the line of  *Hachi: A dog’s tale*.

It's a story of a special force US veteran and his combat companion, a traumatized German shepherd. They both are retired and spending time in the wilds, Alaska, trying to overcome past trauma and loses. In one trip, their private plane crashes in the middle of nowhere. Now injured they need to hike back to civilization but there are other many hurdles including wild lives.

*Brad Pitt* is playing the role of US veteran and he has put fire in the character, as he always do. I am big fan of him so I can be a little biased. The companionship of German shepherd and Brad is so natural that I never felt a single time during the movie that it is a movie. It felt so real. There are scenes where the dog dreams of war and wakes up howling. Brad calms him down so naturally, so beautifully, as if he understands how the dog felt. 

I highly recommend this movie even if you don't like pets, at least for one time watch. If you are  the German shepherd lovers, it's a plus. It deserves **4 stars**
