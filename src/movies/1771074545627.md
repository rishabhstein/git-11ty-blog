This movie is disappointing when compared to other Superman films. You might raise your eyebrows reading this, but based on my limited experience with the DC/Marvel universe, there aren’t many good things in it. On the contrary, I find the use of scientific jargon – such as *pocket universe* – irritating.

Using physics concepts in a movie with such carelessness is outrageous and forces me to question the events that occurred. For example, *Lex Luthor* manages to create his own *Big Bang and pocket universe*, but he somehow forgets to explain how he moves within the universe without an oxygen mask. Perhaps he fills the entire universe with oxygen – LOL! Another problem is aliens moving around Earth’s atmosphere without masks, as if they all breathe oxygen like humans.

I wanted to quit watching in the middle but I finished it. So, overall, it deserves **2** stars.
