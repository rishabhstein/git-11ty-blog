I was in long flight yesterday so I had plenty of time to watch something. I ended up rewatching this movie, and I enjoyed it as much as I did it first time many years ago. It felt refreshing.

The movie is about three engineering students and how the education system force them to memorise science without truly understanding it. Among them, there is a rebellious one who challenges the system. The story line revolves around him, highlighting his success and struggles, which ultimately inspire the other two.

The film is filled with witty remarks about education system, which amused me and made me laugh. I was happy watching it with deep nostalgia. There were also emotional moments on which I was crying.

It deserves **4/5 stars**
