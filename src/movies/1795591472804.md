Following the rise of social network giant, Facebook, this movie shows how it turned from a free speech platform to a radicalizing medium. No one was able to guess when so much power is given to a private company what could go wrong.

In the beginning of the company everyone loved Facebook no matter you was a user or an employee. But when the engagement started declining due to aging user group, Mark Zuckerberg (MZ) decided to manipulate the idea of  free speech  in order to increase so called meaningful social interaction (MSI).  He did this despite being warned that MSI can boost malicious content.

This drastic change happened around 2017-18 and after that whoever tried to warn MZ got threatened, or if it was an employee, got fired. This messed up elections almost everywhere in the world. Fortunately, an employee showed some courage and helped a news reporter to release the internal reports showing what Facebook is doing intentionally.

I wrote about MZ in the book review [Careless People](/books/2026-04-26-book-review-careless-people/) that he seems autistic and does not understand the social complexities much. Yet when such a man holds power, it can lead to disastrous situations. So here we are.

Its a must watch for at least one time. **3 stars**
