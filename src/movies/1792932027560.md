I love watching mountain movies as a part of [armchair travel](https://notes.rishabhps.com/posts/armchait-travel/) fantasies because I don't have enough strength, time, or money to even try climbing above eight thousand meter peaks. This movie fits in both categories—mountains and traveling. It's a story of the famous expedition of Mt. Everest where *Edmund Hillary* and *Tenzing Norgay*  summited the peak successfully for the first time in human history. However, contrary to ignoring Sherpas which most of the movies did in past, this time the storytelling is done from the perception of *Tenzing*.

This movie brings the story of Tenzing who was born in Tibet but later migrated to Nepal with his parents who left him in a village when he got sick during the migratory journey. He grew up as a servant boy, and during his childhood his first encounter with mountaineering was with *George Mallory* who later inspired him to break the shackles of mundane life and climb the unthinkable Goddess, *Chomolungma*, aka Mr. Everest. And whole world knows that he did succeed in it.

I think mesmerizing scenery complemented with beautiful music is the perfect recipe for a mountain movie. This movie combines it with human journey, making it a unique experience for the audience. I highly recommend it with **4 stars**.
