I can describe this documentary/movie in one line: A classic success story turned into a nightmare.

It is about the crappiest dating app ever whose name you can easily guess. Yes **Tinder**.  However, there is a clear feministic touch to it. The protagonist, *Whitney Woolfe* a college graduate works hard to prove that a woman can be as good as a man, especially in tech industry dominated by male nerds. So this female nerd come and show male nerds that she is better than them. She helps them create *Tinder*— wohoo a remarkable job, bravo!! Later they kick her out when one male cofounder harass her.

At this point she realizes that females are really being harassed on *Tinder* so she comes up with another solution to show Tinder guys that they are still no match to her. The solution was *Bumble*, a dating app which priotize female safety.

The documetary ends with following lines (in my words):
1. She in 31 years age is a female millionaire running many dating company
2. She—mark my words—remains married to her husband (remains?? WTF)
3. Blah.. Blah.. Blah..

So all my criticism above is not because she is a female. **I even liked that she showed that she was as smart as any man, a motivation for other girls**. But considering her equal, she showed a common pattern here—college nerds producing a solution to a social problem without understanding the consequences.

Media people, please do not glorify their victory. Promote nice thing in words.

I think it deserves **2/5 stars**
