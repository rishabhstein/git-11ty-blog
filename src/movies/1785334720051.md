Its pretty old movie—thirty four years old, but its songs are still popular. So I must have heard it somewhere, and therefore decided to watch it.

Since it was released three decades ago, it is for a different audience. The movie is about Pakistan invasion of India around 70s. There was a big conflict, and emergency was declared. Unfortunately at the location of invasion there were only 120 soldiers so you can imagine how bloody that fight would be. In the end, India won but lost most of the soldiers in that battalion.

The movie captures the emotion of patriotism with beautiful music. So if you are fan of either, I recommend it with **4 stars**.
