This movie belongs to my favorite genres, autobiography, biography and memoirs. So obviously I was positive about it and wanted to watch it since I saw its trailer last year. 

The movie starts slowly but go deep. War scenes were created from the the photographs of **Lee Miller**, an american woman living in Europe at the time of WWII. She went to frontline during the war and captured in her camera the effected men, women, and childrens. The recreation of those scenes were so surreal that imagining them happening in real time gave me goose bumps.

Ofcourse her all photos were not published because of trauma and horror of that time but seeing them in the form of movie was heavy. Only problem I felt is moments of boredom, especially in the beginning of movie. So I will give it **3.5/5** stars.
