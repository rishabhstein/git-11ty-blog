An inspiring sports movie from Bollywood about the Indian cricket team winning the World Cup in 1983. The players did a remarkable job against all the odds, especially when no one could imagine anything of this sort was possible—before this time, India never reached a semi-final, so the idea of winning the final was improbable.

*Ranbeer Singh* played the real captain *Kapil Dev*.  From walking pace to speaking, he mimicked the real captain nicely. The music score was soothing. I watched this movie multiple times.


**5**⭐.
