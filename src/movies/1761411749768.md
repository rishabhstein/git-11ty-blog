I watched this movie two weeks ago, around 10th October, based on [Benji's](https://www.benji.dog/articles/indieweb-movie-club-october-2025/) recommendation. It is the theme of [IndieWeb Movie Club](https://indieweb.org/IndieWeb_Movie_Club) for October.

This movie is about a few teenagers who are good at computers at the time when computer was just making its first appearance in American homes. It was that golden time of technology when everyone was excited about new technologies. However, these kids start digging deeper into computers and internet, and found loop holes in security. As a result, they start hacking websites/services of government, schools, corporates. Changing grades or courses seems like a *cool* job when its just so easy—hack the website and control it.

Aparrently, they fall into the trap of an evil hacker—working in a big corporation—who use them as a guinea pigs to hide his evil deed. The evil hacker has a puppet in government who tries to catch these kids and blame them for the evil deed. However, the kids are damn smart so they play their techy-tricks and defeat the evil hacker.

The main message is still similar to the message from old moral stories for kids: "Good always wins over evil in the end". However, one crucial point is that these kids are doing hacking, which is modern equivalent of sneaking or stealing. Their motivation might be having fun, but still portraying them as cool modern nerds was a wrong direction director took. It must be entertaining for people at the time of its release but still in my view a wrong direction which completely ignored the moral aspect of the act (hacking). 

We can see the consequences of it nowadays when scammers do not feel any guilt. They are still cool hackers who empty the bank accounts of people.

Another thing is **Angelina Jolie** is the only girl in this nerd group and she is sexualised in all possible way: either in the imagination of her lover or in the intimate scenes with her boyfriend. It was a different time though.

I will give it **3 stars**
