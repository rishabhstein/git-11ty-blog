If you are a fan of Star Wars, and you have seen Disney tv series, **The Mandalorian (2019)**, this movie is going to be a fun. Otherwise, you will find a few aliens fighting with each other, and a tiny baby alien (he is actually 300 years old) doing his cute things.

That cute alien, Grogu, is the key strength of the movie. He is a bit grown up in the movie—compared to the series—and acts as a son of prodigious bounty hunter who belongs to an alien race *Mandalorian*.  The duo has emotional moments which can make you cry.

I recommend it for at least one time watch. **3 stars**.
