This is the second movie I watched in the flight yesterday. Again it was a rewatch. While first one was nice, this one is one of my all time favorite. I have watched it so many times and everytime I watch it, I feel peace.

This movie is about old traditions vs new traditions in *Japan*. Old traditions encourage *ethics and morals* in society while newer ones focus on *power and pleasure*. We all know from our daily experiences that the later is winning due to technological advancement. **Old traditions are dying**. 

**Tom Cruise** is a legend due to his acting as well as movie choices. He knows how to choose a movie and what to do to win hearts. Throughout the movie, he keep the audience engaged by his mysterous personality, which he got from post war PTSD. Adopting older customs (Samurai) helps him recover and find meaning of life.

I think this deserves one of the highest **4.8/5 stars**
