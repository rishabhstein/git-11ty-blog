This movies is a follow up of the previous part of MI movie. It was about an AI algorithm, *Entity*, who becomes self consious and decides to play anti-GOD by wiping out humanity from earth. The hipe was nicely created in the first part and I was eagerly waiting for the second one.

But it was not that charming. **Tom Cruise**, as usually, did his part very well but I felt there were flaws in story line about the plans of future prediciting AI.

There were a few funny moments among which my favorite one was *Hagar, you spend too much time on the Internet!* LOL. It was an action seen where Hagar, a character in Navy, wanted to stop Ethan's plan of destroying the Entity.
