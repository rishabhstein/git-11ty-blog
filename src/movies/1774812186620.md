Journey of a struggling engineering student, who does not want to be an engineer. He quit and very quickly rose as a popular physics teacher. The storyline is seemingly simple, yet his idealism and his action in the struggle of life kept me hooked till the end.

I am sure you will feel inspired in the end. I give it **4 stars**.
