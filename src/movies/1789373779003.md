This movie is impressive due to two reasons: first one is the storytelling in a nostalgic manner with a balanced approach to magic realism. The second one is a strong star cast, especially *Tom Hanks*. His acting is always fascinating due to his ability to deal with complex scenes, with hard emotions. I have seen a very few actors who can do that.

The story revolves around the protagonist, *Paul Edgecomb*, the head of a prison, who witnesses healing powers of a falsely acquitted African man. He learns from his first hand experiences that the African man is innocent but he can not do anything. It is a complicated situation where he is bound to do his duty while it is also wrong to swing the death sentence of an innocent man with divine powers. This dilemma leads to situations which has deep impact on you (the viewer), and for which you should watch the movie.

I loved every minute of the movie. Highly recommend it. **5 stars**.
