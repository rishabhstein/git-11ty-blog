Since the day I started my journey of self-hosting, I had been eagerly waiting for the day when **Plex** would offer a discount on its lifetime subscription. I was so excited about it that there was rarely any week when I didn't check the prices. Finally, last week, I saw the Black Friday deal, which was offering a discount of around a hundred euros. It made me happy, and I immediately started typing the payment details. Then, suddenly, I recalled a [Reddit post]()—which is six to seven years old and I have gone through it many times—where people mentioned that they bought **Plex** for only 75 Euros after a similar discount. 

I checked this post again, and became confused about the price differences because the discount needed to be more than 50% to get that price. So I decided to dig deeper for older black friday deals and resolve this mystery for good.

To my surprise, *Plex* has increased its pricing by a hundred Euros, around the time I started using it, which was in June/July 2025. At that time, I was unsure whether it was so useful that I would buy it one day. So I missed the time of its price increment. :'( 

I felt a bit of sadness, but then I thought, now, based on the current price, I can still save a hundred Euros anyway, so I should hurry up and take it. And, lo and behold, I became a lifetime subscriber to Plex.

I am happy about it. Yeyyyy!!!!

After getting a Plex pass, the first thing I did was make an account for my wife and give her almost full access (only movies and shows, LOL) to my media library. She is thousands of kilometers away, but my Plex Media Server has brought us closer.

Happy Plexing.
