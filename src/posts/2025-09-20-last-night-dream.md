It was a vivid dream. I saw my own baby in the womb as if I were watching him/her live in a 3D scan. However, the feeling was not of a mechanistic view on a screen, but of a real presence. I was there right next to the baby as if it was floating in a transparent film made of jelly like substance. I do not know the gender officially but I felt it to be a male—it’s a bias as I know it from USG scan but officially it’s not disclosed. (S)he had beautiful big eyes which were searching something or may be just observing the surroundings. However, (s)he was not looking at me. The face of the baby was slightly long with a darkish tone of brown skin. (S)he was moving her/his hands as well as the body, lying down on a surface made of biological matter. There was a blur impression of umbilical cord in the background.

While I was watching this entire scene, I was constantly wondering, “How can a baby’s eyes be open in the womb?” It is not possible. But it was just me talking to myself, so there was no answer. Regardless, I felt a sense of peace during whole dream. 

That was it.

Now for the background, it has something to do with my wife’s appointment of *anomaly USG* as she is finishing her 5th month of pregnancy. Her appointment was just after I woke up. Although I was not thinking about it that much as I am far from her right now, I still saw the dream. It was surprising because I do not dream very often.
