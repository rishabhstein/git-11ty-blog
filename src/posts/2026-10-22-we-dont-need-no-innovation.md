Nowadays, new tech gadgets are being released at an increasingly faster pace than ever before.  Every week there is either a new cutting-edge foldable-pro-max-biggest-screen-ever smartphone or a jumping, flying box-like car with revolutionary rocket tech. And do not forget that new AI model which will make your charlatan boss look smarter, and help him cut the budget by firing half of the team. I know I have made many assumptions here, but bear with me. 

Despite these amazing advancements in engineering, I feel less excited and more skeptical with each passing year. I do not understand their purpose or how safe they are, as not much research has been done about their impact. Also, there is a big problem of privacy, where our activities are being tracked.

Perhaps it's my age, as I am getting older (still early thirties, though), or perhaps we as a society fucked up again. I still prefer to take the blame on my age, but why do I then check the launch of new gadgets on YouTube? I think I still look forward to that excitement. And if this is all about age, then why, in these launches, are most of the people older than me (I rarely see a guy in his twenties there)? One example is the new iPhone duo—Jeez, I feel stupefied just thinking about it. I do not understand what is the rush for buying 2-3k $ phone. Maybe your billionaire pocket is so heavy that you can not control it, or you are just an influencer (YouTuber) who wants to snatch it first to get more views. If it's the latter case, dude, you are riding the wave of capitalism just to mess with the minds of young ones. Anyway, none of that impresses me. My refurbished iPhone 13 mini is more than enough to do almost anything. And if I want to appreciate the new technical marvel, I will go to Apple's store, check it out, and then leave.

Fifteen years ago, when I was in my early twenties, I got my first laptop, a Sony Vaio E-series. Then the following year, I got my first LG touchscreen smartphone. Those were a big deal for me, as no one in my family ever used a laptop or a touchscreen phone. At that time, people used to be so surprised/jealous/in-awe seeing a new gadget in someone else's hand that they would come and ask about it. Once, on a train, a girl approached me to check out my new fancy phone. Just thinking about that time makes me excited. The same thing was with cars and motorbikes. My friends and I used to watch automobile shows on Discovery Channel and discuss the specs of new Ferraris or Bugattis, such as engine, acceleration, torque, and top speed. But then something changed that led to the current situation.

I wonder many times about what exactly went wrong. It might be that we passed the peak of development needed to build a prosperous society, and now these new gadgets are just tricks to keep the buzz going. It's similar to a drug addict: we want new shots to feel the same as we felt the first time, but now it's just hurting our health. That is why we see useless and expensive gadgets, pushing the boundaries of biology and psychology just to create a new buzz. AI gadgets, Neuralink chips, AR/XR glasses, and Virtual Reality gadgets are a few more examples here. The worst part is that these techs are advancing so fast that we, as a society, have a hard time studying their impact and building laws for their use cases. The companies behind these fancy tech often claim, with their biased research, that they want to help people with some kind of disability. However, after experiencing the mess they created with social media, it is hard for me to put my trust in them again.

Now in my thirties, I feel my nostalgic moments are guiding me—at least I have those memories, unlike new generations. They remind me that I do not need all those new superhuman gadgets to be happy. I should hold on to whatever I have for now, and reduce my gadget dependence. That does not mean I want to be a tech-hermit, as I love self-hosting and playing with local AI models. I just don't want to spend money for momentarily pleasures in buying stuff, but on my hobbies and health, to gain some satisfaction and peace in life. 

What about you? Do you also feel the same?
