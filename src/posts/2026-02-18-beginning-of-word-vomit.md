In my [previous post](https://https://rishabhps.com/posts/2026-02-15-a-sleepless-night-an-awakening-realization/), I talked about *word-vomit series* where I expressed my desire to make a routine of writing a post every single day. Ofcourse these posts will not be as good as regular blog posts, as I will be forcing myself to write in a limited amount of time. But I do believe that this series will help me to let out the daily thinking chaos.

I am still unsure how I am going to do it. So I plan to mimic [Meadow's style](https://meadow.cafe/blog/0001-setting-wordvomit-goals/), which will save me from some trouble. My first rule is to have a distinct title (such as 0001-title) so that the readers can ignore this shameful series. The second rule is to finish each *word-vomit* post in one sitting. This will be a challenge as I need to find a fixed block of time for writing in my schedule. Irony is that this post itself took three sittings, so you can imagine how hard it's going to be to write a post in one sitting. The third, and the last, rule is to have a target number of words. I am thinking of fixing it up to 500 words, compared to Meadow's 700-word limit.

The reason for a small word target is my limited command of English. Since I am not a native speaker, and I learnt english in schools as a secondary language, it is hard for me to express my thoughts coherently without reviewing. I need relatively more time to write and arrange (and rearrange) the text. I am aware that it's not just my struggle; almost everyone faces it while learning writing, regardless of being a native or non-native speaker.

Another problem in fast and regular writing is my natural inclination toward academic style. Due to the academic training, my self critic is very strong in my head. This critic wants to make sure that there are no mistakes left before pressing the publish button. Hence, the required time to finish the post and publish it gets prolonged.

~~I am at four hundred words, and I am already running out of ideas for what to write.~~ I think this is the actual struggle I need to face and fight to learn writing freely, with less self-scrutiny.
