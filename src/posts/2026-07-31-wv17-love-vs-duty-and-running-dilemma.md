From past few weeks, I have so many thoughts and that's why I have this urge to write a word-vomit post today. I was thinking about multiple topics to write, including Yoga techniques, and many others which I can not recall at this moment. And yet I have not written a single word. I guess the pressure of writing prevents me to write anything at all. The result is me writing word-vomit post which is less pressurizing.

I came back to Brussels after spending a month with my family and six month old son. It was refreshing and calming, with occasional moments of frustrations. Now, sitting thousands of kilometer far, I miss them. There are at least two category of emotions I feel. The first one is about spending time with my family, and live these moments as much as I can because they will never come back. Time is a bitch.

The second type is about responsibility and bringing food on the table. Right now my wife is on a break from work, and my father's business is not doing very well. So all the earning responsibilities are on my shoulder, and that is stressful. My heart wants me to run away to my family and see my son growing up every day, learning new things. While the responsibility demands me to focus on work. Now you might be thinking why not bring your family to Brussels. Well the answer is longer visa-processing-time about which I do not want to talk as this pisses me off further. So that's the problem I am stuck with.

Writing last two lines almost chocked me from the words. So you can see how frustrating this topic is. I will finish this topic with a line from *Game of Thrones* said by *Maester Aemon*:

> Love is the bane of honor, the death of duty.

Moving to another topic, running. I like running for improving cardio health but I struggle with the discipline to do it regularly. I can run every day and build a habit to do it regularly but this can also increase the chances of injury. So, I need to take rest day to recover which eventually breaks the habit. It is confusing. I am struggling with it for around six months now. I might need help.

My [activity calendar](https://blog.rishabhps.com/workout/2026/#month-2026-05) indicates that in past four months the maximum number of runs were in May 2026, exactly nine days. A shame. I expect it to be at least fifteen days. What you can observe from this is the gaps between active (green color) days. When I succeeded in breaking the inertia after rest days, I went for run in following days. However, more often this inertia killed the running streak. So maximum in a week was four, only once.
