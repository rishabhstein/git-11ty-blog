I have not written any long post for almost a month as I felt my writing juices were over. I needed to replenish them to start writing but I guess that will take a long time. Meanwhile, I was posting about books and movies, both of which I am consuming a lot nowadays. Even in these posts, I was either feeling that I have not much to write or the topics are not important. Although, I had occasional motivation to write about different topics such as habits, some skills, and running, for an ADHD mind like mine these bursts did not last long. The moment passed so did the energy of writing. It's a pity as at that time I felt those topics were important but once I forgot them, I did not feel their importance that strongly.

It is an irony to write about not writing again and again, repeating myself countless times [here](/posts/2026-05-09-writing-doubts-and-inspiration/), [here](/posts/2026-04-23-why-i-need-to-take-a-break-in-word-vomit-series/), and [here](/posts/2025-05-18-browsing-vs-writing/). A serious reader would not like it. But do I even have a serious reader? (Sorry for you, if you exist).

Dear reader, I guarantee that it is not intentional. I feel that I have important things to say, especially due to my sharp observant nature. But writing them takes time, and after a day I have plethora of new observations and new ideas. The situation is overwhelming when I am not delivering enough but have more ideas, and higher expectations.

Now you might be expecting that finally I will say something wise or entertaining. Nope. I will not. I want to run away in shame, hide my face under the pillow. Like Mariam did when her husband Rashid tried to hit her with leather belt and Laila (the second wife) hold it and snatched it to prevent the violence. Mariam was scared; so am I. That was from a book I am reading, [*A Thousand Splendid Suns*](/books/2026-09-07-a-thousand-splendid-suns/) by *Khaled Hosseini*.

Regardless of me being wise or boring,  I want to tell you about one of my recent happy moments. I was using Obsidian for quite some time but a few weeks ago it started throwing error on launch. Using Claude, I understood it was a plugin overload. But I was only using 4-5 community plugins. So I was fed up. I decided to quit Obsidian and make my own writing  software where I can save my drafts as well. The best candidate for it was my micro-publishing client. I just needed to work on its writing section. So now  I have this amazing personalized writing page where I can write and save the drafts. So cool. I am happy writing this post in my micropub client. This strategy also has reduced the number of steps I need to publish. Often, the draft in Obsidian stays there and gets abandoned. But here I feel motivated to finish a piece of text, otherwise it will be sitting on my a**, forever.

A side note. My AI usage is growing, and I am okay with it. I mostly use it for writing new codes and developing tools which would have taken me months if done without AI.

Time to go now. See you next time.
