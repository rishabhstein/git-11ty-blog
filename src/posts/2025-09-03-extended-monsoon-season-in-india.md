It's a wonderful rainy day in northern India. The rain is pouring regularly in the late monsoon season when it is expected to be gone. News channels are reporting that this season can extend until mid-September.

Although it's pleasing for me while sitting at home,  the effect of this long monsoon season on the country is very destructive. Dams and reservoirs are at full capacity. Rivers are at the danger mark. Both states, *Himachal* and *Uttarakhand*, in the mountainous region, are struggling with landslides and flooding. Highways are washed away, and there are several casualties. The states in the neighbourhood are also badly affected. Some states, which are known for their dry weather, such as Rajasthan, are also struggling with floods. Everyone is surprised to see such disasters in unexpected places.

Interestingly, most people think of these times as a big inconvenience in their lives. They expect everything to be perfect so that they can lose themselves in entertainment <sup>1</sup> or in the means they get the entertainment. What they do not understand is how our current lifestyle can influence our planet and how, as a consequence, it can disturb the climate.

The only way, in my understanding, is to look into the past weather data and see a clear trend in climate change. That will explain these random changes in the weather, and clear all the doubts of people.

Alas, a common man won't bother because to trust the data and do the fact check requires scientific skills as well as critical thinking. So it seems impossible to educate the masses in a short time, especially when there are other influencing factors, such as politics and online social networks. To make matters worse, the big corps have built such an engaging entertainment that it is hard to come out of it and see reality. The easiest thing to do for people is to **lose themselves** in entertainment and **go with the flow**. If somehow something makes you realize what's really happening, just blame someone else and move on.

I blame the people who are inventing new technology only for the sake of making money. At least *Physicist* takes blame for making the first nuke. But for the other technologies, no one is taking any responsibility. The motto, nowadays, is clear: **Invent and sell**. It does not matter who is buying, who is using, or who is going to be affected.
