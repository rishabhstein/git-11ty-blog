A River Runs Through It

Oct 2, 2025

Tags: #movies

Title
A River Runs Through It
My rating
★★★★☆ 4/5
Watched on
Oct 2, 2025

It took me a while to understand what was extra-ordinary in this movie, and it was its simplicity. The story line is so ordinary that it might remind you just another story at the corner in semi-urban or rural society. Even my cousin's family has a similar story.

This movie reminds me of my young cousin who died a month ago on 14th August in a road accident. He was a rebel. He rests in peace now.

In a small town of North America, Montana, there lives a ordinary family with religious parents and two kids among whom one is rebellious than other. The rebel does not live according to the norms of society while other one adapts those norms. As a result, the rebel lives a brief life—accoding to his own rules and full of excitements—and does not survive long. The other one follows the norm and succeed in life. However, he always miss being a rebel and therefore his brother who eventually becomes a legend.

The character Paul played by Brad Pitt showed this fire of being a rebel. His charms and personality impresses everyone. People love Paul and his boldness, making him a popular journalist. His brother on the other hand is a gentleman who follow the rules.

You can guess the ending now!!

It deserves 4/5 stars.
