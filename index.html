Spartacus (2010)

Oct 29, 2026

Tags: #movies

Title
Spartacus (2010)
Director
Steven S. DeKnight
Location
Brussels, Belgium
Watched on
Oct 27, 2026

It's a story related to a big slave rebellion in Roman republic before Rome became an empire. There was a Thracian soldier (modern day Bulgaria), Spartacus, who served in Roman army for a long time, around ten years, but due to some disagreement with the army general he was punished and pushed into slavery. Later he rose to become a popular gladiator and led fellow slaves to rebel, which lasted for many years and shook Roman republic. His defeat gave a way to the first dictator of Roman empire, Gaius Julius Ceaser.

Now on top of this story, add some sex, nudity, and violence, which is a popular way in modern movie makers to tell Roman history. It does not need to be like this because they can show stoicism, philosophy, and some goodness in people, but it is like this.

I want to point out one special thing in this series. The lead actor, Andy Whitfield, who played Spartacus did it really well but he left after first series because he got cancer. Sadly, he never recovered completely and died. After Andy a new lead actor, Liam McIntyre, took the role of Spartacus but the series could not gain same interest. Liam did a good job in acting but I am not sure what went wrong.

Overall, it's a nice TV series especially for history lover, who can tolerate blood and violence. So I recommend it with 3 stars.
